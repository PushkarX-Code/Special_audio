### New
- Works with every device: headphones, earbuds, speakers, wired, USB and built-in outputs, and any other Bluetooth device
- Battery for every Bluetooth device that reports it, on Windows and Mac
- Every detail the computer knows about the device in Hi-Fi → Device: type, maker, vendor and product ID, firmware, profiles, battery per side and more
- System EQ for every device: 10 bands for everything the computer plays. On Mac it is now built in (macOS 14.2 or later), nothing to install. Save your own curves
- Quick sound presets on Home work with every device
- More than one device connected? Switch between them on Home
- Mac: the visualizer follows the real sound once System Audio Recording is allowed

### Improved
- Noise control, on-device EQ, spatial audio, low lag and button controls show only on devices that have them
