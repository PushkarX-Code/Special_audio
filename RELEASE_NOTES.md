- Custom EQ on the headphones: drag the curve or set Bass, Mid and Treble in 0.5 dB steps, compare with flat, save your own presets. It lives on the headphones, so it follows them to your phone
- PC equaliser (Windows): 10 bands for everything your computer plays, with presets and automatic headroom
- EQ presets, custom EQ, spatial audio and low lag are now on by default
- Developer card in Settings
- Icons on every button, an (i) guide for noise control, and a sidebar that shows when you're connected
